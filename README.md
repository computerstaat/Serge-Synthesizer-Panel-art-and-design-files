# Serge DIY: build Serge-format modules

A DIY resource for Serge-format modular synthesizers. For each module: how it works, where to get its PCB today
(Ken Stone / Elby, Low-Gain, Prism Circuits), which schematic and BOM go with which board, a BOM spreadsheet and Mouser
upload files. Plus panel artwork from 1973 paperface to STS and CGS, with drill templates and Front Panel Designer
(`.fpd`) files for Front Panel Express.

**Website: https://computerstaat.github.io/Serge-Synthesizer-Panel-art-and-design-files/**
· [Build a module](Modules/README.md) · [Mouser BOM files](Modules/BOM/mouser) · [Systems](Systems/README.md) · [Library](Documentation)

> design. It is not a product, it is not affiliated with or endorsed by any of the original designers or

## Folders

The panels are grouped by era:

```
Modern/                 the rounded-box layouts shared by SMMS metal-overlay and STS panels, plus STS-only modules
  Images/               one preview PNG per panel            Images/<category>/<name>.png
  Vector Graphics/      the panel artwork (Inkscape SVG)      Vector Graphics/<category>/<name>.svg
  Front Panel Express/  Front Panel Designer files            Front Panel Express/<category>/<name>.fpd
  Drill Templates/      1:1 printable drill templates (PDF)   Drill Templates/<category>/<name>.pdf
Paperface/              1973–76 panels with the early geometric symbols (paper): Images, Vector Graphics and Drill Templates
SMMS/                   Serge Modular Music Systems, about 1976–92: Paper/ (squared boxes) and Metal_Overlay/ (rounded boxes), in progress
Documentation/          original Serge manuals, catalog and user's guide
CGS/                    Ken Stone's Catgirl Synth (CGS) designs drawn in the Serge style
Systems/                one file per real Serge system (CME 1973, Cal Arts Black Serge, ...) pointing to its panels
```

Each SVG has three layers:

- **Linework**: the print: outlines, lines and labels.
- **Jacks/Knobs**: pictures of the parts, for previewing only.
- **XXX**: drill marks.

## Panel specs

Drill holes (mm):

| Part | Hole |
|---|---|
| Knob | 7 |
| Banana jack | double-D, 8.33 across the round sides, 6.35 across the flats, flats left and right (Cinch 108-09xx) |
| Toggle | 6.35 |
| 1/4" jack | 9.5 |
| LED | 4.25 |
| Push button | 7 |
| Mounting | 3.2 |

The print is the Linework layer as a 600 dpi image, covering the whole plate.

The drill templates print at actual size: every hole at its real diameter with centre cross-hairs, the panel artwork
faintly underneath, and a 50 mm scale bar to check the print came out at 100 %.

Single modules follow the Loudest Warning 4U spec:

- 175 mm high.
- Widths of 25.0 / 50.5 / 75.5 / 101.0 mm for 1–4".
- 3.2 mm mounting holes, 3 mm from the top and bottom edges, NMW − 1" apart and centred.

Quarter panels are 107.95 × 177.8 mm, with 3.2 mm mounting holes 6.35 mm from the sides and 3.175 mm from the top
and bottom.

### Before you order

- Labels are set in Futura Medium Oblique. If your copy renders with a stand-in font, check the labels in the
  artwork before ordering.
- Check every panel against your own parts before drilling: hole sizes, jack type and knob spacing.
