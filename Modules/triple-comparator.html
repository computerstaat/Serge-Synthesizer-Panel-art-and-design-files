How it works

Each section compares two voltages and switches its output high or low according to which is larger. Fed with a waveform and a reference, it produces a square or pulse wave whose width moves as the reference moves.

The reference can be a fixed voltage, a slowly changing control voltage, or another audio signal, so the comparators also serve as level detectors and for logic decisions based on amplitude.
