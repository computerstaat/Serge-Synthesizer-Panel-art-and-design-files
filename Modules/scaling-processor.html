How it works

The top two sections each add one input to a second that passes through a bipolar attenuator, so a signal can be scaled, inverted and offset by another.

The bottom section has two bipolar attenuated inputs, a third input and a -5 to +5 V offset knob; a +5 V jack supplies a fixed voltage for offsets elsewhere in the patch.

Low-Gain's version has three sections: two with one attenuated input and one direct input into a sum, and a bottom section with two attenuated inputs, a -5 / +5 offset and MIN, MAX and SUM outputs, plus a +5V reference jack. The Scaling Processor panel in this repo has no MIN or MAX jacks.

Calibration: trim the offset until the minimum output reads as close to 0 V as you can get.

Low-Gain boards set their own jack positions; check them against the panel you use.

Ken Stone's Serge-derived designs may be built for personal use only; Serge Tcherepnin keeps all rights to his circuits. Schematics, parts lists and PCBs belong to their designers and sellers, so these pages link to them rather than copying them.
