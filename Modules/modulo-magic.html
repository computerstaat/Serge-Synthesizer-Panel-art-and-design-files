How it works

Like the remainder after a division: with a 1 V step, the output follows the input from 0 to 1 V, then drops back by a step and follows again. Step initiation and step size are set by knobs and voltage.

On control voltages it turns a ramp into repeating patterns or staircases; on audio it folds waveforms into bright, buzzy harmonics like a wave multiplier.

Bill of materials

Spreadsheet: modulo-magic.csv (CSV; opens in Excel, Numbers or Google Sheets). It lists the parts for every build option: Ken Stone's parts lists, and the parts data from Low-Gain's, Prism's and Elby's own BOMs where they publish one.

Order from Mouser: modulo-magic__elbykit_cgs340.csv (20 lines, about $24.25 at single-unit prices). Files ending _cart are made from a maker's own Mouser cart. Upload one at Mouser's BOM tool (it reads the Mouser and manufacturer part number columns). Parts Mouser doesn't stock are listed in modulo-magic__elbykit_cgs340_not_at_mouser.csv.

Check stock and price before ordering: 5 lines in this file (marked [UNVERIFIED] in the Description column) use part numbers built from the manufacturers' numbering and not yet checked on mouser.com; their prices are estimates. The BOM tool flags anything it can't find or that is out of stock.

Pots and knobs from Tayda: modulo-magic__elbykit_cgs340.csv (1 lines, about $3.36). The pots (16 mm, solder lugs, 1/4" round shaft) and Davies 1900H-style knobs cost less at Tayda Electronics than at Mouser, so they are listed here with a link to each Tayda product instead of in the Mouser file.

Or genuine Davies knobs from Mouser: the Tayda knob is a Davies 1900H clone ($0.42). If you want the real Davies Molding 1900-H (Mouser 5164-1900-H, about $4.36 each; 5164-1900-L has an indicator line), upload knobs/modulo-magic__elbykit_cgs340.csv (8 knobs) at Mouser's BOM tool instead and leave the knob line out of the Tayda order.
