TKB · Touch Activated Programmer Sequencer

Sixteen capacitive touch plates that play as a keyboard or step through as a sequencer, each stage holding four rows of voltages.

How it works

Each of the 16 stages has a touch plate and four knobs (rows A to D). Touching a plate, or a pulse at its stage select input, makes that stage active: its four voltages appear at the row outputs and a gate goes high.

Played by hand it is an expressive keyboard with a pressure output; clocked, it steps through the stages forwards or backwards, and stage outputs patched back to stage inputs set the length and order of the sequence.

AEP gives a pulse on every stage change and CPO a gate while any plate is held, so it can trigger envelopes whether played or sequenced.
