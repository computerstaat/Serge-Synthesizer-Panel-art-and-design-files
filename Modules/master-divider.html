How it works

Each stage halves the frequency of the one before. Every division also has outputs shifted by a quarter cycle, so events can land on the off-beat or lag deliberately.

Driving several sequencers from one clock through it keeps them in step at related speeds, for example one sequencer at an eighth of the other's rate.
