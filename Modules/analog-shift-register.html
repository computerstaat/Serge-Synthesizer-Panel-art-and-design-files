Analog Shift Register

ASR · CGS34

A three-stage bucket-brigade sample-and-hold: each clock pulse passes every held voltage on to the next output.

How it works

On each clock pulse the first stage samples the input, and each stage hands its held voltage to the next. The outputs give the current value and the two before it, so a single melody turns into a three-part canon.

A built-in gate-to-trigger converter gives its own pulse output for clocking other modules. Some resistor pairs need matching for accuracy.

Bill of materials

Spreadsheet: analog-shift-register.csv (CSV; opens in Excel, Numbers or Google Sheets). It lists the parts for every build option: Ken Stone's parts lists, and the parts data from Low-Gain's, Prism's and Elby's own BOMs where they publish one.

The makers' parts lists were copied into the spreadsheet automatically from their PDFs and Mouser carts (values, quantities and reference designators only; each row links its source). Check them against the maker's current BOM before ordering, and read the maker's notes there: they are not repeated here.

Order from Mouser: analog-shift-register__cgs34_asr.csv (19 lines, about $29.25 at single-unit prices), analog-shift-register__prism_cart.csv (25 lines, about $45.05 at single-unit prices). Files ending _cart are made from a maker's own Mouser cart. Upload one at Mouser's BOM tool (it reads the Mouser and manufacturer part number columns). Parts Mouser doesn't stock are listed in analog-shift-register__cgs34_asr_not_at_mouser.csv, analog-shift-register__prism_cart_not_at_mouser.csv.

Makers' Mouser carts: Prism Circuits Analog Shift Register: Prism Mouser cart (the Prism BOM). Each opens the maker's shared Mouser project, where you can add the whole list to your cart. They are kept by the makers, so they follow the current board revision, but some of their parts were out of stock or obsolete on 21 Nov 2026. The _cart upload files above are the same lists with those parts swapped for in-stock equivalents, so use them if Mouser shows a part as backordered.
