How it works

Each section sums three inputs, each through its own level knob, into one output. The top section's switch inverts or mutes its first channel, useful for cancelling or comb-filtering two copies of a signal.

The lower section has an AUX IN that joins its output bus directly, so mixers can be chained.

LGE209 schematic

One section per board: three AC-coupled inputs (the third with a phase switch) and one output. To make it DC-coupled, remove C8, C9 and C10 and fit jumpers in their place.

There is no AUX IN jack, so the board will not fill every hole on the Serge Audio Mixer panels in this repo.

The DIY PCBs come with the surface parts fitted; you add the panel parts and the power header.

Low-Gain boards set their own jack positions; check them against the panel you use.
