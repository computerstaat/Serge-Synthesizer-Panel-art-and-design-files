# Tayda parts lists: pots and knobs

For the Ken Stone boards, the front-panel pots and their knobs are cheaper at Tayda Electronics (https://www.taydaelectronics.com) than at Mouser, so they are listed here instead of in the Mouser files. Each `<module>__<board>.csv` has the Tayda SKU, quantity, price and a link to the product page; add the SKUs to your cart there (Tayda's Quick Order takes SKU and quantity).

Pots: Alpha-type 16 mm, solder lugs, 6.35 mm (1/4") round shaft, so they take 1/4" knobs such as the Davies 1900H clone listed with them (one knob per pot). Values Tayda doesn't stock in that style (20K linear) stay in the Mouser file, with the knob still listed here. Prices were checked on 2026-11-21 and change; check stock before ordering.
