How it works

Two signals, X and Y, are multiplied. With both as audio the output holds their sum and difference frequencies: the metallic, bell-like ring-mod sound. With one as a slow control voltage it becomes a VCA.

Two VC inputs move the module smoothly between full ring modulation and plain amplitude modulation. DC coupling lets it process control voltages as well as audio.

The same main board is built as either a Ring modulator or a Gate (VCA), each with its own panel and I/O board (LGE006RC or LGE006GC). Only the Ring version lines up with the Ring Modulator panels in this repo.

V1 boards need trace cuts, plus a further mod for Gate / VCA use; V2 boards need neither.

Low-Gain warns that a negative voltage at the linear CV input can drive the VCA to full rail level; keep the CV attenuators in use.

Low-Gain boards set their own jack positions; check them against the panel you use.

Ken Stone's Serge-derived designs may be built for personal use only; Serge Tcherepnin keeps all rights to his circuits. Schematics, parts lists and PCBs belong to their designers and sellers, so these pages link to them rather than copying them.

Bill of materials

Spreadsheet: ring-modulator.csv (CSV; opens in Excel, Numbers or Google Sheets). It lists the parts for every build option: Ken Stone's parts lists, and the parts data from Low-Gain's, Prism's and Elby's own BOMs where they publish one.

The makers' parts lists were copied into the spreadsheet automatically from their PDFs and Mouser carts (values, quantities and reference designators only; each row links its source). Check them against the maker's current BOM before ordering, and read the maker's notes there: they are not repeated here.

Order from Mouser: ring-modulator__cgs79_ring.csv (19 lines, about $12.57 at single-unit prices), ring-modulator__prism_cart.csv (22 lines, about $19.12 at single-unit prices). Files ending _cart are made from a maker's own Mouser cart. Upload one at Mouser's BOM tool (it reads the Mouser and manufacturer part number columns). Parts Mouser doesn't stock are listed in ring-modulator__cgs79_ring_not_at_mouser.csv, ring-modulator__prism_cart_not_at_mouser.csv.

Makers' Mouser carts: Prism Circuits Ring Modulator: Prism Mouser cart (the Prism BOM). Each opens the maker's shared Mouser project, where you can add the whole list to your cart. They are kept by the makers, so they follow the current board revision.

Pots and knobs from Tayda: ring-modulator__cgs79_ring.csv (2 lines, about $0.81). The pots (16 mm, solder lugs, 1/4" round shaft) and Davies 1900H-style knobs cost less at Tayda Electronics than at Mouser, so they are listed here with a link to each Tayda product instead of in the Mouser file.
