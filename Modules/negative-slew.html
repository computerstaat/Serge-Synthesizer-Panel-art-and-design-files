Negative Slew

CGS82

Two sections that slew falling voltages, and with a little patching become voltage-controlled sawtooth oscillators.

How it works

Each section lets a rising input pass straight through but slows the fall, at a rate set by the knob and its voltage control: a lag on negative-going steps.

Patch the section's pulse output back into its input and it regenerates, running as a voltage-controlled sawtooth oscillator, from slow LFO up to audio.

The LGE082 is two negative slopes plus a Hills & Valleys (min / max) section on one 6" x 2" board with the LGE082C I/O board for the V3 white panel. The single-slope paperface Negative Slew panel in this repo has room for neither the second slope nor the min / max jacks.

Bill of materials

Spreadsheet: negative-slew.csv (CSV; opens in Excel, Numbers or Google Sheets). It lists the parts for every build option: Ken Stone's parts lists, and the parts data from Low-Gain's, Prism's and Elby's own BOMs where they publish one.

The makers' parts lists were copied into the spreadsheet automatically from their PDFs and Mouser carts (values, quantities and reference designators only; each row links its source). Check them against the maker's current BOM before ordering, and read the maker's notes there: they are not repeated here.

Order from Mouser: negative-slew__cgs82_negative_slew.csv (24 lines, about $18.51 at single-unit prices), negative-slew__prism_cart.csv (28 lines, about $46.81 at single-unit prices). Files ending _cart are made from a maker's own Mouser cart. Upload one at Mouser's BOM tool (it reads the Mouser and manufacturer part number columns). Parts Mouser doesn't stock are listed in negative-slew__cgs82_negative_slew_not_at_mouser.csv, negative-slew__prism_cart_not_at_mouser.csv.

Makers' Mouser carts: Prism Circuits Negative Slew: Prism Mouser cart (the Prism BOM). Each opens the maker's shared Mouser project, where you can add the whole list to your cart. They are kept by the makers, so they follow the current board revision.

Pots and knobs from Tayda: negative-slew__cgs82_negative_slew.csv (1 lines, about $1.68). The pots (16 mm, solder lugs, 1/4" round shaft) and Davies 1900H-style knobs cost less at Tayda Electronics than at Mouser, so they are listed here with a link to each Tayda product instead of in the Mouser file.
