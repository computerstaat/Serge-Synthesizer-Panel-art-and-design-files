How it works

Each section lets a rising input pass straight through but slows the fall, at a rate set by the knob and its voltage control: a lag on negative-going steps.

Patch the section's pulse output back into its input and it regenerates, running as a voltage-controlled sawtooth oscillator, from slow LFO up to audio.

Known error: in the Hills & Valleys section R57 is wired to ground; its ground end should go to V- (Low-Gain links a photo of the fix).

The LGE082 is two negative slopes plus a Hills & Valleys (min / max) section on one 6" x 2" board with the LGE082C I/O board for the V3 white panel. The single-slope paperface Negative Slew panel in this repo has room for neither the second slope nor the min / max jacks.

Low-Gain boards set their own jack positions; check them against the panel you use.
