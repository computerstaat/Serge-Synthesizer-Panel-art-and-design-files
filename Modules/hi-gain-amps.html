How it works

Each amplifier raises a weak external signal (a microphone, contact mic or guitar) to the level the rest of the system expects, so it can be filtered, enveloped and used to trigger events.

The lower sections of the panel are passive multiples for splitting signals.

One preamp with 60 dB of gain per board; the paperface panel has room for several, so order one per amplifier you fit and wire the rest as multiples.
