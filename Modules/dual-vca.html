How it works

Each channel is a voltage-controlled amplifier with an exponential (audio-taper) response, so an envelope sounds even across its whole range.

The VC input sets gain together with the knob; near zero the output drops quickly to silence. Gain tops out at about two, so the channels can also boost weak signals before a filter or waveshaper.

Low-Gain bases it on the original Serge 2VCA, with matched SMD transistors.

Six jacks and two GAIN knobs (IN, CV, OUT and GAIN per channel), the same as the Serge 2VCA panel. To DC-couple, jumper C12 and C14. Calibration notes were still marked 'coming soon' on 21 Nov 2026.

Low-Gain boards set their own jack positions; check them against the panel you use.

Ken Stone's Serge-derived designs may be built for personal use only; Serge Tcherepnin keeps all rights to his circuits. Schematics, parts lists and PCBs belong to their designers and sellers, so these pages link to them rather than copying them.
