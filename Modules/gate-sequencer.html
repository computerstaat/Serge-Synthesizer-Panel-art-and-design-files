How it works

A counter steps through the stages on each clock. A switch at each stage sends that stage's gate to one of the output rows or leaves it silent, giving rhythmic patterns across several outputs.

This is Ken Stone's smaller-format version; the original paperface Gate Sequencer is a different panel (photo still needed).

Low-Gain's is a 10-stage decade-counter gate sequencer: Clock, Reset, Reset/Hold and Hold inputs, one gate output per stage, Manual Advance and Reset/Hold push buttons and bi-colour stage LEDs. The CGS Gate Sequencer panel in this repo has stage switches and row outputs instead, so the LGE089C I/O board does not fit it.

Parts inside the white outline on the main board are only for the Gate Sequencer XL and can be left off. V1 I/O boards need the manual-advance modification shown on the product page.

Low-Gain boards set their own jack positions; check them against the panel you use.

Ken Stone's Serge-derived designs may be built for personal use only; Serge Tcherepnin keeps all rights to his circuits. Schematics, parts lists and PCBs belong to their designers and sellers, so these pages link to them rather than copying them.

Bill of materials

Spreadsheet: gate-sequencer.csv (CSV; opens in Excel, Numbers or Google Sheets). It lists the parts for every build option: Ken Stone's parts lists, and the parts data from Low-Gain's, Prism's and Elby's own BOMs where they publish one.

The makers' parts lists were copied into the spreadsheet automatically from their PDFs and Mouser carts (values, quantities and reference designators only; each row links its source). Check them against the maker's current BOM before ordering, and read the maker's notes there: they are not repeated here.

Order from Mouser: gate-sequencer__cgs89_gate_sequencer.csv (23 lines, about $267.47 at single-unit prices). Files ending _cart are made from a maker's own Mouser cart. Upload one at Mouser's BOM tool (it reads the Mouser and manufacturer part number columns). Parts Mouser doesn't stock are listed in gate-sequencer__cgs89_gate_sequencer_not_at_mouser.csv.
