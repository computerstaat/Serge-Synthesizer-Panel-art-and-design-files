How it works

A voltage follower whose rise and fall times are set separately, by knobs and by voltage. A signal at INPUT comes out slewed; a trigger at TRIG IN fires a full rise-and-fall envelope.

With CYCLE on, the END pulse retriggers the slope, so it runs as an LFO or, with the 1V/OCT input, as a roughly tracking oscillator that sweeps from triangle to sawtooth as rise and fall change.

The same circuit acts as a portamento, an envelope follower, a pulse delay or sub-harmonic divider, and a rough low-pass gate on audio.

Bill of materials

Spreadsheet: vc-slope.csv (CSV; opens in Excel, Numbers or Google Sheets). It lists the parts for every build option: Ken Stone's parts lists, and the parts data from Low-Gain's, Prism's and Elby's own BOMs where they publish one.

Order from Mouser: vc-slope__cgs75_vcs.csv (40 lines, about $53.54 at single-unit prices). Files ending _cart are made from a maker's own Mouser cart. Upload one at Mouser's BOM tool (it reads the Mouser and manufacturer part number columns). Parts Mouser doesn't stock are listed in vc-slope__cgs75_vcs_not_at_mouser.csv.

Pots and knobs from Tayda: vc-slope__cgs75_vcs.csv (3 lines, about $3.66). The pots (16 mm, solder lugs, 1/4" round shaft) and Davies 1900H-style knobs cost less at Tayda Electronics than at Mouser, so they are listed here with a link to each Tayda product instead of in the Mouser file.

Or genuine Davies knobs from Mouser: the Tayda knob is a Davies 1900H clone ($0.42). If you want the real Davies Molding 1900-H (Mouser 5164-1900-H, about $4.36 each; 5164-1900-L has an indicator line), upload knobs/vc-slope__cgs75_vcs.csv (5 knobs) at Mouser's BOM tool instead and leave the knob line out of the Tayda order.
