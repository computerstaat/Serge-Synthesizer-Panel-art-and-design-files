How it works

A low-pass gate is a low-pass filter that closes completely when its control voltage is at zero, so it behaves like a VCA that also darkens the sound as it fades.

Vactrols (an LED and photocell) do the gating, and their slow release gives the natural decay that makes short pulses sound like plucked or struck instruments. The mode switch picks combined filter-and-VCA or low-pass only.

Based on the Buchla 292 with two VTL5C3 vactrols per gate and extra circuitry against vactrol bleed; COMBO and LOW-PASS modes only (no VCA mode).

The paperface board is one gate; order two for this dual panel. The LW set is already dual.
