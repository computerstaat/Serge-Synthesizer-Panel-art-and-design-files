Quantizer

Turns any control voltage into equal-tempered steps: chromatic, whole tone, augmented triads or a six-step major-minor scale.

How it works

A 0 to 5 V ramp at an input comes out as a staircase of 1V/octave semitones, accurate to 3 cents over five octaves, with about 8 ms response.

Two scale-select inputs change the step size: 1/6 gives a whole-tone scale, 1/3 major thirds, and both together alternate half steps and minor thirds. Because the selects can switch quickly, the scale can change within a phrase.
