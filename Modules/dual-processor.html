How it works

Each half sums three input voltages. Each input has a level control that also sets polarity, so a voltage can be added, reduced or inverted.

A manual offset adds a fixed voltage to the result. Use it to combine envelopes and LFOs, transpose a sequence, or turn a 0-5 V signal into a bipolar one.

Bill of materials

Spreadsheet: dual-processor.csv (CSV; opens in Excel, Numbers or Google Sheets). It lists the parts for every build option: Ken Stone's parts lists, and the parts data from Low-Gain's, Prism's and Elby's own BOMs where they publish one.

The makers' parts lists were copied into the spreadsheet automatically from their PDFs and Mouser carts (values, quantities and reference designators only; each row links its source). Check them against the maker's current BOM before ordering, and read the maker's notes there: they are not repeated here.

Order from Mouser: dual-processor__lowgain_cart_lge012.csv (16 lines, about $28.51 at single-unit prices), dual-processor__prism_cart.csv (12 lines, about $44.31 at single-unit prices). Files ending _cart are made from a maker's own Mouser cart. Upload one at Mouser's BOM tool (it reads the Mouser and manufacturer part number columns). Parts Mouser doesn't stock are listed in dual-processor__lowgain_cart_lge012_not_at_mouser.csv, dual-processor__prism_cart_not_at_mouser.csv.

Makers' Mouser carts: Prism Circuits Dual Processor: Prism Mouser cart (the Prism BOM); Low-Gain LGE012 Dual Processor: LGE012 Main Board. Each opens the maker's shared Mouser project, where you can add the whole list to your cart. They are kept by the makers, so they follow the current board revision, but some of their parts were out of stock or obsolete on 21 Nov 2026. The _cart upload files above are the same lists with those parts swapped for in-stock equivalents, so use them if Mouser shows a part as backordered.
