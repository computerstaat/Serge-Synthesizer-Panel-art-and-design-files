How it works

Each clock pulse moves the sequencer one stage on. The active stage puts out a pulse (and lights its LED), so the stages fire other modules in turn: envelopes, routers, sample-and-holds or a Programmer.

RESET returns it to the first stage and HOLD freezes it, so sequences of fewer than ten steps and stop-start patterns are easy. Prism's calibration sets the step output to 5-6 V.
