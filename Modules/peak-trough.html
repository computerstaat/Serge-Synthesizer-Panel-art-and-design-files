How it works

Each side takes several voltages at once. PEAK outputs whichever input is highest at each moment; TROUGH outputs whichever is lowest.

With gates these act as OR and AND; with audio or slow voltages they combine shapes in ways a mixer cannot, such as clipping one waveform with another or picking the louder of two envelopes.

LGE084 V2 error fix

Known errors: on V1, R5 in the Hills / Valleys circuit goes to ground where it should go to V-. On V2, a ground trace shorts the Peak (Hills) OR bus on the top half of the board; cut the trace on the underside as shown on the product page. V2.1 has its own BOM, schematic and layout.

A single 6" x 1" board with no I/O board listed.

Low-Gain boards set their own jack positions; check them against the panel you use.

Bill of materials

Spreadsheet: peak-trough.csv (CSV; opens in Excel, Numbers or Google Sheets). The Mouser columns are there to fill in with order links.

Order from Mouser: peak-trough__cgs84_peakandtrough.csv (10 lines, about $8.66 at single-unit prices). Upload it at Mouser's BOM tool (it reads the Mouser and manufacturer part number columns). Parts Mouser doesn't stock are listed in peak-trough__cgs84_peakandtrough_not_at_mouser.csv.

Makers' Mouser carts: Prism Circuits Peak & Trough: Prism Mouser cart (the Prism BOM). Each opens the maker's shared Mouser project, where you can add the whole list to your cart. They are kept by the makers, so they follow the current board revision.

Pots and knobs from Tayda: peak-trough__cgs84_peakandtrough.csv (2 lines, about $1.62). The pots (16 mm, solder lugs, 1/4" round shaft) and Davies 1900H-style knobs cost less at Tayda Electronics than at Mouser, so they are listed here with a link to each Tayda product instead of in the Mouser file.
