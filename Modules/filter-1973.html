How it works

A state-variable filter: one circuit produces low-pass, band-pass and high-pass versions of the input at the same time, each on its own output. All three share one cutoff frequency and one resonance (Q).

The cutoff is set by a knob and by voltage control; Q sets how strongly the filter rings at the cutoff. The original uses CA3080 transconductance amplifiers for the voltage-controlled stages.

Prism's calibration starts with a sawtooth into the audio input and the cutoff fully down, listening at the low-pass output.

Bill of materials

Spreadsheet: filter-1973.csv (CSV; opens in Excel, Numbers or Google Sheets). It lists the parts for every build option: Ken Stone's parts lists, and the parts data from Low-Gain's, Prism's and Elby's own BOMs where they publish one.

The makers' parts lists were copied into the spreadsheet automatically from their PDFs and Mouser carts (values, quantities and reference designators only; each row links its source). Check them against the maker's current BOM before ordering, and read the maker's notes there: they are not repeated here.

Order from Mouser: filter-1973__cgs77_vcf.csv (37 lines, about $32.14 at single-unit prices), filter-1973__prism_cart.csv (34 lines, about $54.71 at single-unit prices). Files ending _cart are made from a maker's own Mouser cart. Upload one at Mouser's BOM tool (it reads the Mouser and manufacturer part number columns). Parts Mouser doesn't stock are listed in filter-1973__cgs77_vcf_not_at_mouser.csv, filter-1973__prism_cart_not_at_mouser.csv.

Makers' Mouser carts: Prism Circuits 1973 VCF: Prism Mouser cart (the Prism BOM). Each opens the maker's shared Mouser project, where you can add the whole list to your cart. They are kept by the makers, so they follow the current board revision, but some of their parts were out of stock or obsolete on 21 Nov 2026. The _cart upload files above are the same lists with those parts swapped for in-stock equivalents, so use them if Mouser shows a part as backordered.

Pots and knobs from Tayda: filter-1973__cgs77_vcf.csv (3 lines, about $3.35). The pots (16 mm, solder lugs, 1/4" round shaft) and Davies 1900H-style knobs cost less at Tayda Electronics than at Mouser, so they are listed here with a link to each Tayda product instead of in the Mouser file.

Or genuine Davies knobs from Mouser: the Tayda knob is a Davies 1900H clone ($0.42). If you want the real Davies Molding 1900-H (Mouser 5164-1900-H, about $4.36 each; 5164-1900-L has an indicator line), upload knobs/filter-1973__cgs77_vcf.csv (4 knobs) at Mouser's BOM tool instead and leave the knob line out of the Tayda order.
