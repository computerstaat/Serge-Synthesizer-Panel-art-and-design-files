How it works

The Smooth section slews any voltage in both directions under voltage control: lag, portamento, or a low-frequency filter. Patch its CYCLE jack to its input and it oscillates as a voltage-controlled triangle oscillator.

The Stepped section samples its input and holds the value, moving in steps at a rate set by its own control.

The COUPLER links the two halves. Coupled to each other they make smooth or stepped random voltages, staircase waves and many other patterns, which is why the SSG is one of the most patched Serge modules.

Bill of materials

Spreadsheet: smooth-stepped-generator.csv (CSV; opens in Excel, Numbers or Google Sheets). It lists the parts for every build option: Ken Stone's parts lists, and the parts data from Low-Gain's, Prism's and Elby's own BOMs where they publish one.

The makers' parts lists were copied into the spreadsheet automatically from their PDFs and Mouser carts (values, quantities and reference designators only; each row links its source). Check them against the maker's current BOM before ordering, and read the maker's notes there: they are not repeated here.

Order from Mouser: smooth-stepped-generator__cgs92_ssg.csv (33 lines, about $35.04 at single-unit prices), smooth-stepped-generator__lowgain_cart_lge092.csv (38 lines, about $48.88 at single-unit prices), smooth-stepped-generator__prism_cart.csv (35 lines, about $65.46 at single-unit prices). Files ending _cart are made from a maker's own Mouser cart. Upload one at Mouser's BOM tool (it reads the Mouser and manufacturer part number columns). Parts Mouser doesn't stock are listed in smooth-stepped-generator__cgs92_ssg_not_at_mouser.csv, smooth-stepped-generator__lowgain_cart_lge092_not_at_mouser.csv, smooth-stepped-generator__prism_cart_not_at_mouser.csv.

Makers' Mouser carts: Prism Circuits Smooth & Stepped Generator: Prism Mouser cart (the Prism BOM); Low-Gain LGE092 Smooth Stepped Generator: LGE092 V1.3 Main & I/O Board. Each opens the maker's shared Mouser project, where you can add the whole list to your cart. They are kept by the makers, so they follow the current board revision.

Pots and knobs from Tayda: smooth-stepped-generator__cgs92_ssg.csv (1 lines, about $1.68). The pots (16 mm, solder lugs, 1/4" round shaft) and Davies 1900H-style knobs cost less at Tayda Electronics than at Mouser, so they are listed here with a link to each Tayda product instead of in the Mouser file.

Or genuine Davies knobs from Mouser: the Tayda knob is a Davies 1900H clone ($0.42). If you want the real Davies Molding 1900-H (Mouser 5164-1900-H, about $4.36 each; 5164-1900-L has an indicator line), upload knobs/smooth-stepped-generator__cgs92_ssg.csv (4 knobs) at Mouser's BOM tool instead and leave the knob line out of the Tayda order.
