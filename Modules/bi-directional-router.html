How it works

Each section is an electronic switch that works in both directions: A1 can be routed to A2 or B, or either can be routed back to one jack. A pulse or a control voltage above a threshold flips it.

Use it to alternate notes between two voices, swap modulation sources on a beat, or build simple sequential patches. Prism's calibration sets the internal rails to +7 V and -7 V with the trimmers.

Documents
LGE024 V2 BOM

The LGE024 has four routers, not three, and each has an ON / OFF / EXT toggle and a bi-colour LED next to its CV input. The Triple Bi-Directional Router panels in this repo have three channels and no toggles or LEDs, so the LGE024C I/O board will not line up with them.

The current boards are V2; Low-Gain asks you to email for V1 sets.

Low-Gain boards set their own jack positions; check them against the panel you use.

Ken Stone's Serge-derived designs may be built for personal use only; Serge Tcherepnin keeps all rights to his circuits. Schematics, parts lists and PCBs belong to their designers and sellers, so these pages link to them rather than copying them.

Bill of materials

Spreadsheet: bi-directional-router.csv (CSV; opens in Excel, Numbers or Google Sheets). It lists the parts for every build option: Ken Stone's parts lists, and the parts data from Low-Gain's, Prism's and Elby's own BOMs where they publish one.

The makers' parts lists were copied into the spreadsheet automatically from their PDFs and Mouser carts (values, quantities and reference designators only; each row links its source). Check them against the maker's current BOM before ordering, and read the maker's notes there: they are not repeated here.

Order from Mouser: bi-directional-router__cgs88_bds.csv (15 lines, about $11.81 at single-unit prices), bi-directional-router__prism_cart.csv (15 lines, about $30.32 at single-unit prices). Files ending _cart are made from a maker's own Mouser cart. Upload one at Mouser's BOM tool (it reads the Mouser and manufacturer part number columns). Parts Mouser doesn't stock are listed in bi-directional-router__cgs88_bds_not_at_mouser.csv, bi-directional-router__prism_cart_not_at_mouser.csv.

Makers' Mouser carts: Prism Circuits Bi-Directional Router: Prism Mouser cart (the Prism BOM). Each opens the maker's shared Mouser project, where you can add the whole list to your cart. They are kept by the makers, so they follow the current board revision, but some of their parts were out of stock or obsolete on 21 Nov 2026. The _cart upload files above are the same lists with those parts swapped for in-stock equivalents, so use them if Mouser shows a part as backordered.
