How it works

The three inputs are DC coupled and summed into one output. Inputs 1 and 2 have ordinary attenuators; input 3's knob is bipolar, so at the left it inverts the signal, at the centre it is off and at the right it passes it at full level.

The top knob adds a fixed offset of -5 to +5 V to the sum, so the module can scale, invert and shift an envelope or LFO, transpose a pitch voltage, or act as a manual voltage source with nothing patched.
