How it works

A Serge analog noise core gives white and pink noise outputs. The same noise drives a random voltage section that produces a stepped (held) voltage and a smoothed version of it, changing at a rate set by the RATE knob and VC RATE input; PULSE gives a pulse at each change.

A separate sample-and-hold takes whatever is patched to S/H SOURCE (or the internal noise) and holds it on each trigger at TRIG; TRIG I/O passes the timing pulses in or out, and the button version adds a manual trigger.
