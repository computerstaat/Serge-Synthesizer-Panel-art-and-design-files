Panel builder

Pick modules and get one panel: a single SVG with every module side by side, ready to print, to send to a panel maker, or to open in Inkscape. Each module keeps its layers, including the drill marks.

Modules are 7" tall (Loudest Warning format, 175 mm). Check the result against your own jacks, pots and case before drilling; module widths come from each module's artwork. Mixing eras works, but the styles will differ.
Serge DIY: build notes, parts lists and panel artwork for Serge-format modules, shared for educational purposes. Schematics, PCBs and BOMs belong to their designers and sellers and are linked, not copied. Module designs, names and trademarks belong to their owners. Source on GitHub.
