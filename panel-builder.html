Build a panel

Each module has its own build page; a panel is a set of modules side by side. Pick modules and get the whole panel: a single SVG with every module side by side, plus a 1:1 drill template and a Front Panel Express kit for the whole panel.

Download

  • Artwork: one SVG for the whole panel, with Background, Linework and Jacks/Knobs layers from each module, plus one XXX layer of drill marks for the whole panel. The modules' own drill marks and mounting crosses are left out.
  • Drill template: every hole of the whole panel at drill size, to print at 100% and tape to the blank. A 50 mm bar checks the scale. Tiled sheets overlap by 15 mm and are numbered.
  • Parts for this panel: a Mouser BOM-tool upload file and a Tayda list (pots and knobs, with links) for each chosen module, so each can be checked on its own, plus the same parts added up for the whole panel (Ken Stone boards with their sub-boards; two of a module doubles the parts) and the parts neither shop stocks.
  • Front Panel Express kit: the hole list, the print artwork and the build script. Open Front Panel Designer, double-click Build panel, and it saves a ready-to-order .fpd file. Details are in the kit's README.
Modules sit as close together as on the M-Class and 17" panels: about 2.2 mm between module outlines. Modules are 7" tall (Loudest Warning format, 175 mm). Check the result against your own jacks, pots and case before drilling; module widths come from each module's artwork. Mixing eras works, but the styles will differ.
