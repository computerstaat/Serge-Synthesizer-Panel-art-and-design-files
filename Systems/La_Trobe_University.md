# La Trobe University

The paperface system at La Trobe University, Melbourne, specified by Warren Burt and later restored by Ken Stone. Four panels, LA1–LA4.

Era: **Paperface**.

Photos: the system laid out flat with its panels marked LA-1 to LA-4, and later photos of it mounted in a
wooden frame (four rows of three panels) in a studio. The same 12 panels appear in both: the three
Programmer-style panels across the top, the panel with a white insert in the second row, and the row of
twelve 1" Glide strips in the middle.

Status: **not built yet.** Photos confirm these paperface modules in the system: Noise Source (1": WHITE / PINK / S/H SOURCE pairs), Glide (1" strips, twelve in a row), Bi-Directional Quad-Switch (2"), Sequencer (with a LINK SEQ TO PROGRAMMER switch), Mixer (3 columns), Phaser, Analog Shift Register, Ring and Gate. Most labels in the photos are too small to read, so the panel-by-panel line-up still needs mapping.

The Reverb and Adapter modules seen on the La Bestia reproduction are not part of the original system and are left out.

## Identified modules

- **Top-left panel, second module: Ten-Stage Pulse Sequencer** (identified by the user, Nov 2026). It is labelled SEQUENCER on the panel. Each of the ten stages is a pair of red jacks with a numbered LED between them: stages 1-5 run up the left column and 6-10 down the right, so 1 sits beside 10 at the bottom and 2 beside 9 above it. Along the bottom is a row of triangle-boxed inputs, with CLOCK first and RESET last; RESET has a red push button. The two middle labels can't be read in the photo. The Catgirl Synth "Sequencers" panel uses the same stage pair, LED and RESET / HOLD / CLOCK scheme.
- A close-up of another panel shows a Mixer (inputs 2-4 with x1 outputs), the Triple Wave Shaper (VC1 / VC2) and the Phaser (1080 / 720 / 360, MIX, the + knob).
