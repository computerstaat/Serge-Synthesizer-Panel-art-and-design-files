# Warren Burt Serge / Driscoll system

Warren Burt moved from the US to Australia in 1975 to teach at La Trobe University. He put together three Serge systems over about ten years; this is the last of them, built between 1977 and 1983. It started as four panels and had grown to twelve by 2013, when Jon Dent (djjondent) bought it. The panels were restored by Andrew F of NonLinearCircuits.

The twelve panels are nine Serge panels plus three black panels by Julian Driscoll. They sit in four wooden boxes of three panels each, arranged two boxes over two.

## Box 1 (top left): the earliest panels

- **Top panel:** yellow paperface with coloured jacks. Left to right: 2 Oscillators, 4 VCAs, Triple Wave Shaper, and the Phaser (with two hand-drawn ducks saying "QUACK!"). Then 2 Dual Analog Shift Registers on aluminium, the SMMS metal overlay style marked "serge".
- **Middle panel:** white SMMS metal overlay. 3 New Timbral Oscillators, Wave Multipliers, the original Serge VC Filter (high / band / low pairs), the Extended Range VCF, and a jack and adapter strip. The footer reads "SERGE MODULAR MUSIC SYSTEMS".
- **Bottom panel:** white handmade panels with hand-written labels: Interface Convertors, 2 handmade VC low-pass (ladder) filters, and 2 dual 3-in / 1-out Mixers. On the right are 2 yellow paperface Dual Processors.

## Box 2 (top right)

- **Top panel:** yellow paperface. 2 Dual Positive Slews, 2 Dual Negative Slews, 2 Smooth Function / Stepped Function VC Generators, the VC Slew Generator, and Peak & Trough. The Peak & Trough has a hand-written note: "AARDVARK'S HOME COMPANION - W.B. 1977".
- **Middle panel:** yellow paperface. 2 Envelope Generators, the Bi-Directional Router, the Comparator, and the Schmitt Trigger.
- **Bottom panel:** white handmade (Warren Burt). 8× interface connectors, 2 joysticks, MIC preamp, Ring Modulator (X, Y, OUT RM), 2 Additive Programmers, Quad Pan with Q FADE INS / OUTS, and the original VC Filter.

## Box 3 (bottom left): Julian Driscoll panels

These three panels are by Julian Driscoll, who designed and built about 10 to 15 units at La Trobe University between 1977 and about 1982. Burt says the filters were Serge clones; Josue Arias says they share the Serge topology but are not exact clones. The modules are marked "JCD 79", and some carry penguin stickers.

The art style is completely different from Serge's: black panels with thin white line art.
- **Inputs:** jacks inside circles.
- **Audio inputs:** diamonds.
- **Outputs:** squares.
- **Knobs:** numbered scales (0-10, or -5 to +5 for processors).
- **Labels:** small mixed-case, for example "vc Q", "Glide vc", "init. Q".
- **Section dividers:** thick white rules.

1. **Voice panel:**
   - 2 DSLOPE (each a pair of slope generators: 1V/Oct, RISE and FALL knobs from .1mS to 20 m., TRIG, IN, CYCLE, OUT).
   - 2 ASR (each two ASRs: IN, TRIG IN, 4 OUTPUTS, TRIG OUT).
   - 2 VCO 1: SIN, TRI, SAW, VARI, PULSE, 1V/OCT, Sync, Glide and FM, VARI vc, Glide vc, FM vc, Pul W vc, init. Glide, init. FM, VARI SHAPE, AUX Processor, Fine Tune, Pulse Width, AUX vc, NOR / LO switch, TUNE.
   - A coloured jack strip.
2. **CV processors and filter panel:**
   - 4 CV PROCESSORS: IN1, IN2, IN3, OUT, OFFSET, UGIN.
   - 3 VCF 1: LOW, HIGH, NOTCH, BAND, IN1, IN2, TRIG, 1v/oct, vcQ, vcF, init. Q, vcF processor, NOR / LO, TUNE.
   - STEREO MIXER.
   - TRIPLE SCALER.
   - 4 VCAs (DVCA).
3. **Sequencer panel:**
   - 5 × 32 STAGE SEQUENCER: stages 0-31 in two columns, RESET, CLOCK, and an I/P or O/P switch. These are probably the divide-by-32 counters Driscoll built to Burt's specifications.
   - VCO 3 DUAL OSC: two oscillators, each with SQU, SAW, TRI, 1V/OCT, Processor, Fine, TUNE, aux vc.
   - VCO 1.
   - RVG: In, COMP Out T, level vc, Out L, LEVEL, S/H In / Out / Samp, LF, NOISE Wht / Pnk, GLIDE In / Out / glide vc.

## Box 4 (bottom right): white SMMS metal overlay panels, hand-decorated with cartoons

1. **Top panel:** Programmer, Sequencer, Programmer, Sequencer (the user identifies these sequencers as the Ten-Stage Pulse Sequencer layout), plus a module with a cartoon cat.
2. **Middle panel:** a jack strip / multiples module, Wilson Analog Delay, Resonant Equalizer, Variable Slope VCF, 2 Dual Transient Generators, Precision VCO, Dual VCA.
3. **Bottom panel:** Touch Activated Keyboard Sequencer (TKB): 16 stages with A-D knob rows and touch plates. The Catgirl "Touch Activated Programmer Sequencer" panel art is drawn after this. Jon Dent's TKB rebuild is documented at the link above (CGS / Human Comparator TKB).

## Status

Catalogue only; nothing is drawn yet. The Driscoll panels would need their own art style in the builder: black panel, white lines, circles, diamonds and squares, and numbered knob scales.
