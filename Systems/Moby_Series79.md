# Moby's SMMS "Series 79"

A two-panel SMMS system with a Touch Activated Keyboard Sequencer, sold on Reverb from Moby's collection and listed as
an SMMS Series 79. The line-up comes from the listing photos.

Era: **SMMS metal overlay** (rounded boxes, black on silver), around 1979–80.

| Panel | Modules (left to right) | Artwork |
|---|---|---|
| Top | Precision VCO ×2, Random Source, Audio Mixer, Dual VCA, Dual Universal Slope Generator, Dual Phaser, Dual Channel Stereo Mixer | [PNG](../Modern/Images/Full%20Panel/Moby_Series79_Panel_1.png) · [SVG](../Modern/Vector%20Graphics/Full%20Panel/Moby_Series79_Panel_1.svg) · [Drill](../Modern/Drill%20Templates/Full%20Panel/Moby_Series79_Panel_1.pdf) |
| Bottom | Analog Shift Register, New Timbral Oscillator, Ring, Wave Multipliers, Variable Q VCF, Variable Slope VCF, Dual Transient Generator, Active Processor, Preamp Detector | [PNG](../Modern/Images/Full%20Panel/Moby_Series79_Panel_2.png) · [SVG](../Modern/Vector%20Graphics/Full%20Panel/Moby_Series79_Panel_2.svg) · [Drill](../Modern/Drill%20Templates/Full%20Panel/Moby_Series79_Panel_2.pdf) |
| Keyboard | Touch Activated Keyboard Sequencer | [PNG](../Modern/Images/Full%20Panel/Touch_Activated_Keyboard_Sequencer.png) |

The panels are drawn with the repo's existing module artwork. Small differences from this system's panels:
- its Analog Shift Register uses yellow jacks for AUX PULSE and PULSE IN;
- its Dual Universal Slope Generators have a dark red LED in each OUTPUTS box;
- its Precision VCOs have yellow SYNC jacks.
