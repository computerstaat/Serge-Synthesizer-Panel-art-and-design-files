# CME La Jolla, 1973

The paperface system built for the Center for Music Experiment (CME) at UC San Diego, La Jolla, in 1973. One of the panels carries the line "CENTER FOR MUSIC EXPERIMENT — LA JOLLA, CALIFORNIA — 1973". The line-up comes from photos of the six panels and their Wikipedia captions; the module drawings follow the 1973 Paper Face Modules First User's Guide in `../Documentation`.

Era: **Paperface** (1970s line art). Six 17" panels.
