# Serge Modular Music

The middle era of Serge panels: white or cream printed panels with upright sans-serif labels and
"SERGE MODULAR" or "SERGE MODULAR MUSIC SYSTEMS" along the bottom edge. They sit between the 1970s
paperface panels (`../Paperface`) and the later black-text style (`../Modern`).

Nothing has been drawn for this era yet. A module is only added once a photo confirms the
Serge Modular Music version of its panel.

Once panels are added, the folder follows the same layout as `Modern`:

```
Images/<category>/<name>.png
Vector Graphics/<category>/<name>.svg
Drill Templates/<category>/<name>.pdf
```
