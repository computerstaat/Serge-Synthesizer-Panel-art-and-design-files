Moby's SMMS "Series 79"

A two-panel SMMS system with a Touch Activated Keyboard Sequencer, sold on Reverb from Moby's collection and listed as an SMMS Series 79. The line-up comes from the listing photos.

Era: SMMS metal overlay (rounded boxes, black on silver), around 1979–80.

The panels are drawn with the repo's existing module artwork. Small differences from this system's panels: - its Analog Shift Register uses yellow jacks for AUX PULSE and PULSE IN; - its Dual Universal Slope Generators have a dark red LED in each OUTPUTS box; - its Precision VCOs have yellow SYNC jacks.
