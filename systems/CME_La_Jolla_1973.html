CME La Jolla, 1973

The paperface system built for the Center for Music Experiment (CME) at UC San Diego, La Jolla, in 1973. One of the panels carries the line "CENTER FOR MUSIC EXPERIMENT — LA JOLLA, CALIFORNIA — 1973". The line-up comes from photos of the six panels and their Wikipedia captions; the module drawings follow the 1973 Paper Face Modules First User's Guide in ../Documentation.

Era: Paperface (1970s line art). Six 17" panels.

Where it went

This is the system Ken Stone later owned and documented as his "1973 classic Serge". Warren Burt assembled it for CME; it was then taken to Australia for the Clifton Hill Community Music Centre, later belonged to Rainer Linz (who used it in Stelarc performances), and came to Ken Stone in a simple wooden frame with loose panels. Ken Stone's panel line-up matches the six panels above.

Unlike most Serges it was built with 3.5 mm jacks, chosen at CME by Roger Reynolds because he thought banana plugs too flimsy. The artwork here keeps the repo's banana jacks.

Notes:

  • The Dual Mixer on these panels is the 4" version; the 3" Dual Mixer is a separate drawing.
  • Jacks are drawn as black banana jacks throughout; the originals used silver for audio.
