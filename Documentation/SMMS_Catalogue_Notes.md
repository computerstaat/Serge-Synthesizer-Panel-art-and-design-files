# What the SMMS catalogues tell us

## The two box styles

- **1979 catalogue (Series 79):** every panel drawing has square-cornered boxes, the module name sits inside the top of the outline, and "serge" is printed in the bottom edge. This is the **squared** SMMS style.
- **1980 catalogue:** the same modules redrawn with rounded boxes and the name above the outline. This is the **rounded** SMMS style. The drawings are the same ones as in the STS catalogue that the Modern set was drawn from, so the Modern (aluminium) panels kept the rounded SMMS layout.

So a module's layout can be checked against the 1980 drawings for both rounded SMMS and Modern, and against the 1979 drawings for squared SMMS.

## Catalogued but not drawn yet

Quad VCA, the 4" UPAP, Multi-Channel Stereo Mixer and its Dual Stereo Panner channel, Multi-Channel Quadraphonic Mixer and its Quad Panner channel, the 3" Dual Audio Mixer, Four-Input Stereo Mixer/Panner, Envelope Detector and Dual Envelope Detector, the 2" VC Phaser, Pitch and Envelope Follower, Ten-Stage Pulse Sequencer, 5-, 7- and 8-stage Sequencer Programmers, the 1" Quantizer for SQPs, N-Voice Controller and Dual Random Voltage Generator.

These are SMMS modules. Under the era rule they go into `SMMS/` from the catalogue drawings, and into `Modern/` only once a photo shows an aluminium version.

Other notes:
- The Pitch and Envelope Follower is a Serge catalogue product (7"), even though the panels in photos carry Gentle Electric's name.
- The 1979 COM is the Triple Comparator (2"); by 1983 COM is a 1" Dual Comparator.
- The 1979 catalogue's Ring Modulator is a 2" "Ring Modulator / VC Gate"; by 1983 it is the 1" Ring Modulator.
